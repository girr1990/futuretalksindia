DEEP DIVE · IP ASSET 02

The Agentic Maturity Model, at a glance.

Before you touch a single AI tool, you need to know where you are starting and where you are going. The AMM is your map. Here is the full L1 to L5 spectrum, with a real example at each level.

L1
Human-Driven
Everything is manual. Decisions live in people's heads. Tools are passive. No AI in the loop.
REAL EXAMPLESLA tracking in a shared Excel file, updated manually every Friday.
L2
Assisted
AI helps you think faster. Claude drafts, ChatGPT summarizes, Gemini suggests. You still review and decide everything.
REAL EXAMPLEPaste exception data into Claude, ask it to spot patterns, validate and act.
L3
Semi-Automated
AI handles standard cases. A no-code app routes routine work. You only touch exceptions and edge cases.
REAL EXAMPLEAn AI agent classifies invoice exceptions. You see only the ones it flags as uncertain.
L4
Agent-Driven
Agents execute end to end. They communicate with each other. You set policy, review exceptions, monitor performance.
REAL EXAMPLEAn agent resolves standard exceptions, logs actions, notifies stakeholders, escalates true anomalies.
L5
Autonomous
Self-learning systems. They adapt, retrain, and optimize their own logic. You set strategy and boundaries.
REAL EXAMPLEA predictive SLA model that retrains itself monthly and adjusts its own alert thresholds.
